# Season Zero Roster Verification Pack

## Purpose

Operations-safe Game Day roster reference — name, Ultra Athlete ID, jersey
number, and position only. No email, phone, application notes, medical
notes, or emergency contact information appears here or should ever be
added to this document.

## Verification note

Pulled read-only from production immediately after the Onitolo/Yinka/Rofiat
Secondary Draft picks and the Hawau/Mbah/Adeshina roster correction — 67
total rostered players across 8 clubs (32 men's, 35 women's). Cross-check
against `/gameday` club readiness before the event; this is a snapshot.

**Known gap, not fabricated here:** jersey numbers are not currently
populated for any player in production. Leave `#` blank on the printed sheet
rather than assigning one on the fly unless the league has a real jersey
assignment to record.
