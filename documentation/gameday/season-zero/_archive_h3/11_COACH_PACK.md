## Reserve/surplus coaches

None auto-assigned. Per the H.1 spec this system was built against: do not
assign a reserve coach automatically. If a club's head coach becomes
unavailable, that's an Event Director decision on the day, handled the same
way a player becomes unavailable — through check-in/incident tools, not a
silent roster change.
