# Product Philosophy

## Philosophy

Technology should disappear behind creativity.

Users should make creative decisions—not technical ones.

## Core Principles

1. Story before technology.
2. Character before spectacle.
3. Creative intent before prompts.
4. Continuity by default.
5. AI is a production crew, not the storyteller.
6. Progressive disclosure.
7. Provider independence.
8. One Creative Engine for children and professionals.

## Decision Framework

Every feature should improve storytelling, reduce cognitive load, preserve continuity, and remain provider-independent.
