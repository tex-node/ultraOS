# Season Zero Launch Readiness

Ultra Basketball Season Zero launch date: August 15, 2026.

Production configuration:

- Competition: Ultra Basketball
- Season: Season Zero 2026
- Timezone: Africa/Lagos
- Currency: NGN
- Divisions: Men's Division, Women's Division

Launch blocker classes:

- P0: Cannot launch.
- P1: Launch only with commissioner approval.
- P2: Can launch with documented workaround.
- P3: Post-launch improvement.

Examples of P0 blockers:

- Database migration failure.
- Authentication failure.
- Draft allocation integrity failure.
- Public display cannot recover after refresh.
- Backup unavailable.
- Duplicate player squad assignment.
- Payment marked successful without provider verification.
- QR tickets expose predictable identifiers.
- Required officials missing.
- Venue, internet, or power unavailable.

Go-live rule:

- GO_LIVE_READY is blocked while any P0 readiness check remains open.
- P1 blockers require explicit commissioner approval and documented workaround.
- P2 blockers require named owner, workaround, and post-event follow-up.
